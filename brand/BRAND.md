# Private Pattern — Brand Rules (distilled for the post engine)

Custom elevated casual tailoring. Fabric milled in Italy, crafted in Vietnam.
A private members' club for men — wooden cigar lounge, luxury locker room.
Heritage warmth, after-hours intimacy, brass fittings, walnut walls.

This file is the single source the engine (and any AI captioner) reads for
voice and visual adherence. Keep it in sync with `voice.json` and `tokens.css`.

## Mission

- **Purpose** — Modernize made-to-measure: take a slow, intimidating,
  expert-only process and make it effortless, personal, and built for how
  busy men actually live.
- **Vision** — Redefine "Made in Vietnam": prove Vietnamese craftsmanship
  belongs at the top of menswear.

## Values (content pillars)

| Pillar | Essence |
| --- | --- |
| **Craft** | Take the making seriously. Quality is never the thing that gets cut. |
| **Ease** | Remove every decision possible. Never make him think hard. Ease is also the tailoring term: room built into a garment so it moves. |
| **Brotherhood** | A crew, not a customer base. Members arrive by nomination, through someone they trust. The clothes are serious; the people are not. |
| **Intention** | Nothing is accidental. If there is no reason for it, it is removed. The Vietnamese word is *tỉ mỉ*. |
| **Heritage** | Made in Vietnam is the headline, not the disclaimer. |

## Voice

A tailor speaking in confidence: low, declarative, slightly arch.
Editorial, never marketing-speak. Reads like a calling card, not a CTA.

- The brand says *we*; the member is *you*.
- Title Case for headlines and plaques (*Your Pattern*, *Nominations*).
- ALL CAPS, wide-tracked, only for micro-labels and selvage ribbons.
- Sentence case for body copy. Punctuation conventional and quiet.
- Single upright words with a full stop carry status ("Unfound.").

### Never

- No emoji. No exclamation points.
- No "Get yours today", no urgency, no discounts language.
- No buzzwords: *revolutionary, seamless, game-changer, unlock, elevate
  your wardrobe, luxury for less, limited time, don't miss*.
- "Bespoke" only when earned, never as filler.
- No testimonials-grid energy, no feature waffle.

## Visual system (what the templates implement)

- **Substrate** — walnut `#120b07 → #4a3020`; cards are translucent walnut.
  Morning theme is parchment `#f5edc8` with walnut ink `#281a0e`.
- **Accent** — aged brass only: `#b8976a`, bright `#d4b079`, deep `#8c6932`.
  No semantic colors. Pure white never; cream `#f0e4c9` stands in for ivory.
- **Type** — Copperplate for every heading (engraved caps, tracked positive).
  JetBrains Mono for body, micro-labels, ledger numerals. Emily Austin script
  only for accents/flourishes — never body, never labels.
- **Logo** — masked SVG filled cream with a 1px brass keyline (engraved
  treatment). Monogram (favicon mark) for small placements.
- **Frames** — gilt double-fillet: 1px outer hairline, 4px breathing room,
  1px inner rule. Hairlines at 35% alpha, never opaque borders.
- **Corners** — sharp. 0–3px radius. No pills except the monogram badge.
- **Texture** — film grain overlay, quarter-sawn wood-grain stripes,
  amber lamp glow from upper-left, functional vignettes only.
- **Ornaments** — unicode glyphs as iconography: ✻ · № ← ✓ &
- **Selvage ribbon** — `Private Pattern ✻ Drape Club · Fabric Milled in
  Italy ✻ Crafted in Vietnam` in wide-tracked caps between gold hairlines.
- **Layout** — centered editorial composition, generous negative space,
  48px gold under-bar beneath heading rules (the signature detail).
